Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit or interact with the website of Sunil Kumar, Advocate.

Last updated: September 2026

01

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the website available at advsunilkumar.in and describes how information may be handled when you visit the website or voluntarily provide information through its contact facilities.

The website is intended to provide information about the professional practice, areas of legal work, professional background and contact details of Sunil Kumar, Advocate.

02

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, information that may be provided or processed can include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number, if you choose to provide it.
  • The subject or general nature of your enquiry.
  • Any information you voluntarily include in a message submitted through a contact facility.

The website is not intended to require visitors to provide sensitive identity documents or detailed case records merely to make an initial enquiry.

03

How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily provided through the website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to an enquiry.
  • Communicating with you regarding your request.
  • Understanding the general nature of the enquiry so that it can be appropriately directed or responded to.
  • Maintaining the security and proper operation of the website.

Information submitted through the website is not intended to be used for unrelated marketing purposes.

04

Contact Enquiries

If a contact form is provided on this website, information submitted through that form may be transmitted to the designated professional recipient through the third-party service used to process the submission.

The website is intended to use contact submissions for initiating communication. It is not intended to operate as a case-management system or electronic case file.

Please do not submit confidential or highly sensitive information through a general website enquiry form.

Initial enquiries should contain only the information reasonably necessary for making contact. Further information can be discussed through an appropriate professional channel if the matter is taken forward.

05

Cookies & Technical Data

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, performance or measurement.

Depending on the hosting, analytics or other technical services used by the website, certain technical information may also be processed automatically. This may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate usage information, referring pages and server or security logs.

Any analytics or optional tracking technology introduced in the future should be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

06

Third-Party Services & Links

The website may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, form processing, email delivery, analytics, security or other technical purposes.

The website may also contain links to external websites, including courts, legal resources, professional profiles, publications or other third-party sources.

External websites operate under their own privacy practices and policies. Visitors should review the privacy information applicable to those websites before providing information through them.

07

Data Retention

Information submitted through the website will generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, for responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate records, complying with applicable obligations, or protecting the security and operation of the website.

Actual retention periods may depend on the service providers used to process or store the information.

08

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information handled through the website against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, visitors should avoid submitting information through a general web form that they would not ordinarily send through an unsecured communication channel.

09

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information when contacting the website. However, some information may be necessary to respond to an enquiry, such as a means of contacting you.

If you have questions about information that you have submitted through the website, you may contact the website owner using the contact details provided below.

10

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically toward children. Visitors should not submit personal information belonging to a child through the website unless they are authorised to do so and the information is reasonably necessary for the enquiry.

11

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, its technical services, applicable requirements or information-handling practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
