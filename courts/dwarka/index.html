Dwarka Courts

A Delhi District Court complex that the official history associates with the South-West District and IGI Airport area.

Complex history and recorded area

The Delhi District Courts’ official history records that construction of Dwarka Courts Complex was completed in 2008 and that it was inaugurated on 6 September 2008. That account associates the complex with cases pertaining to the South-West District and IGI Airport.

Confirm the current forum

The district and area reference is taken from the official history page, whose allocation note is not dated as a current court directory. It should not be used alone to decide where a new or pending case belongs. Confirm the current assignment and applicable jurisdiction with an up-to-date official source.
