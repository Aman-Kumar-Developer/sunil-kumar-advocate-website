Saket Courts

A Delhi District Court complex that began functioning in 2010 and took up specified work previously handled at Patiala House.

A change recorded in 2010

The Delhi District Courts’ official history states that Saket Court Complex began functioning on 28 August 2010. It records that specified civil and criminal work relating to South Civil District and the South and South-East Police Districts—previously handled at Patiala House—was taken up at Saket. It also notes that Motor Accident Claim Petitions for the relevant police-district areas were handled by Motor Accident Claims Tribunals (MACTs) established there.

Use current case information

The official account documents the arrangements when the complex began functioning; it is not a dated current directory of every case category or allocation. A historical description cannot determine the proper venue of an individual case. Confirm current assignment and jurisdiction using up-to-date official court information.
