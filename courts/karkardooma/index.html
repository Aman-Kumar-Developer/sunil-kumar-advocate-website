Karkardooma Courts

A Delhi District Court complex with a documented history of serving court proceedings transferred from Shahdara.

A District Court complex in Delhi

The Delhi District Courts’ official history records that the Karkardooma Courts Complex was inaugurated on 15 May 1993 and that courts functioning at Shahdara were shifted there. This establishes a significant point in the complex’s history; the history page does not set out a current district-by-district case-allocation directory.

Confirming the appropriate court

The correct court and court complex for a particular proceeding depend on the applicable territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction and the current assignment of the case. The historical account cited here should not be treated as confirmation that a specific new matter belongs at Karkardooma.
