Tis Hazari Courts

A long-established Delhi court complex with a prominent place in the recorded history of the District Courts.

A landmark in Delhi District Court history

The Delhi District Courts’ official history records that construction of the Tis Hazari Courts building began in 1953 and that it was inaugurated on 19 March 1958. The account describes the building as having housed all civil courts and many criminal courts at that time and identifies it as a principal Delhi court building in the history it presents.

Historical context is not a current court list

The history page describes earlier court arrangements; it does not provide a dated, current list of the matters or districts assigned to Tis Hazari. A particular proceeding’s proper court and location should be confirmed against its current assignment and applicable jurisdiction.
