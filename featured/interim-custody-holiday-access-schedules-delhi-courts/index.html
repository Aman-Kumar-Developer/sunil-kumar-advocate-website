Interim Custody & Holiday Access Schedules in Delhi Family Courts

Procedural framework under Section 12 of the Guardians and Wards Act governing school vacations, weekend overnight access, and festival visitation arrangements.

This page summarizes the legal framework and judicial precedents for educational and informational purposes only. It does not state or imply that Advocate Sunil Kumar Upadhyay represented a party or was involved in the matter.

Interim Protection under Section 12 Guardians and Wards Act

Under Section 12 of the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890, Family Courts possess wide jurisdiction to pass interlocutory orders for the temporary custody and protection of a minor child during the pendency of main guardianship proceedings.

Interim custody applications are urgent: protracted procedural delays risk alienating the child from the non-custodial parent, creating entrenched emotional distances that prejudice final custody determination.

Structuring Weekend, Holiday, and Festival Schedules

Delhi Family Courts routinely implement structured graduated access plans: (i) supervised contact at court children's rooms or mediation centres, progressing to (ii) unsupervised day outings on alternate weekends, and (iii) overnight weekend access from Friday evening to Sunday afternoon.

For school vacations (summer, autumn, and winter breaks), courts typically divide the vacation period equally between parents. Major festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Eid are either shared or alternated annually to enable the child to celebrate with both extended families.

Balancing School Routines and Child Well-Being

Access orders prioritize the child's academic schedule, extracurricular activities, and health. Orders routinely specify pickup and drop-off protocols, prohibited travel restrictions, and guidelines preventing either parent from disparaging the other in the child's presence.

Key Precedents & Authorities

  • Guardians and Wards Act, 1890, Section 12 — India Code
  • Family Courts Act, 1984 — India Code
