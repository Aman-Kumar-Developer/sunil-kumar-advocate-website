Title Verification and Due Diligence in Delhi Real Estate

Essential legal checks covering 30-year encumbrance certificates, sub-registrar record searches, mutation verification, and local zonal master plan clearances.

This page summarizes the legal framework and judicial precedents for educational and informational purposes only. It does not state or imply that Advocate Sunil Kumar represented a party or was involved in the matter.

The 30-Year Title Chain and Sub-Registrar Search

Title verification in Delhi real estate requires establishing an unbroken, marketable chain of title extending back at least thirty years. Counsel must inspect registered documents at the concerned Sub-Registrar's office, verifying conveyances, sale deeds, gift deeds, and release deeds.

A comprehensive search across Book 1 and Book 2 registers ensures that the property is free from undisclosed mortgages, court attachments, lis pendens notices, and statutory charges.

Suraj Lamp Doctrine: Invalidity of GPA / Agreement to Sell Transfers

In the landmark ruling Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Haryana (2012) 1 SCC 656, the Supreme Court unequivocally held that immovable property can only be transferred by a registered deed of conveyance. Transactions conducted via General Power of Attorney (GPA), Special Power of Attorney (SPA), and Will do not convey ownership title.

In Delhi, properties held on GPA tenure require meticulous examination regarding conversion to freehold under DDA / MCD policies before any valid registered conveyance can be executed.

Master Plan Compliance and Mutation Records

Due diligence must also verify land usage under Master Plan for Delhi (MPD 2021 / MPD 2041), verifying whether the structure conforms to building sanctioned plans, completion certificates, and FAR norms.

Furthermore, mutation records in municipal registers (MCD property tax assessment records) must be matched against registered title deeds, noting that mutation is merely for fiscal tax purposes and does not create independent proprietary title.

Key Precedents & Authorities

  • Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. v. State of Haryana (2012) 1 SCC 656 — Indian Kanoon
  • Registration Act, 1908, Section 17 — India Code
