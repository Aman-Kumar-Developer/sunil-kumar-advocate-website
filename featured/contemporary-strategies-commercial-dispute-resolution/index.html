Contemporary Strategies in Commercial Dispute Resolution

An overview of evolving dispute management strategies combining early case evaluation, structured mediation, and expedited commercial court litigation.

This page summarizes the legal framework and judicial precedents for educational and informational purposes only. It does not state or imply that Advocate Sunil Kumar Upadhyay represented a party or was involved in the matter.

Early Case Assessment & Pre-Litigation Due Diligence

Modern commercial litigation demands rigorous pre-litigation evaluation. Commercial entities and counsel must scrutinize contractual risk allocations, notice requirements, limitation timelines, and evidentiary records before filing claims.

Proper pre-action assessment helps identify whether a matter warrants urgent interim measures, institutional mediation under Section 12A, or rapid invocation of an arbitration clause.

Summary Judgments under Order XIII-A CPC

The Commercial Courts Act, 2015 introduced Order XIII-A to the Code of Civil Procedure, empowering commercial courts to dispose of claims without recording oral evidence where the defendant has no realistic prospect of succeeding on the defence, or the plaintiff has no prospect of succeeding on the claim.

Strategic utilization of Order XIII-A allows commercial claimants with clear documentary proof (invoices, acknowledgment of debts, bank records) to secure rapid decrees without enduring multi-year full trials.

Strict Case Management Timelines under Order XV-A

Under Order XV-A, Commercial Courts hold mandatory Case Management Hearings to streamline trial schedules, frame issues, and set hard deadlines for witness cross-examination.

Furthermore, the 120-day forfeiture rule for filing written statements affirmed in SCG Contracts (India) Pvt. Ltd. v. K.S. Chamankar Infrastructure (2019) 12 SCC 210 eliminates dilatory tactics, necessitating swift and disciplined litigation defence.

Key Precedents & Authorities

  • SCG Contracts (India) Pvt. Ltd. v. K.S. Chamankar Infrastructure (2019) 12 SCC 210 — Indian Kanoon
  • Commercial Courts Act, 2015 — India Code
