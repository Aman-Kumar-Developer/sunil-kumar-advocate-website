Banking · Debt recovery

Banking & Recovery Litigation

Legal assistance in disputes relating to loans, recovery, debt enforcement and other banking issues, with attention to the documents and proceedings relevant to each matter.

Overview

The practice includes banking and recovery disputes, loan recovery, debt enforcement and banking-related litigation. Depending on the dispute and applicable law, related work may involve the SARFAESI framework or proceedings before a Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT).

Matters and legal assistance

  • Advice and representation in loan recovery and banking disputes.
  • Review of relevant loan, security and recovery documents.
  • Assistance in debt-enforcement matters, including SARFAESI-related issues where applicable.
  • Representation in DRT proceedings where the matter and instructions call for it.

Matter-specific considerations

The documents, procedural stage, parties and relief sought all matter. The availability of a particular remedy or forum depends on the facts and applicable statutory requirements; a general website description cannot determine the position in an individual dispute.

Legal assistance

Assistance may include reviewing the available record, explaining relevant options and representing a client in appropriate proceedings. The scope of work is determined after understanding the matter; no outcome is guaranteed.
