Civil, Commercial & Contractual Matters

Advice and representation in civil disputes, commercial litigation, contractual matters and business-related legal issues.

Overview

Civil and commercial disputes can arise from agreements, business dealings or other legal relationships. The professional practice includes civil disputes, commercial litigation, contractual matters and business-related legal issues.

Matters and legal assistance

  • Advice and representation in civil disputes.
  • Commercial litigation and business-related legal issues.
  • Contractual disputes and questions about obligations under agreements.
  • Review of relevant records and preparation for appropriate proceedings.

Understanding a dispute

The terms of any agreement, the communications and records, the parties’ positions and the relief sought can shape the available options. Early review can help clarify the issues and whether negotiation, another dispute-resolution process or court proceedings should be considered.

Legal assistance

Work is considered in light of the documents and circumstances of the particular dispute. Advice and representation are matter-specific; this page does not predict an outcome or describe an individual case.
