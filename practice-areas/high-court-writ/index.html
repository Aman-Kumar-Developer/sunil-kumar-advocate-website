PRACTICE AREAS

Delhi High Court · Public law

Constitutional & Writ Practice

Advice and representation in writ petitions and constitutional matters before the Delhi High Court, where appropriate to the facts and legal issues.

Overview

The professional practice includes constitutional matters and writ proceedings before the Delhi High Court. A public-law concern does not automatically establish that a writ petition or a particular remedy is available.

Matters and legal assistance

  • Advice on issues raised in constitutional and public-law matters.
  • Preparation and representation in writ petition proceedings where appropriate.
  • Review of relevant decisions, records and supporting documents.
  • Discussion of the legal issues, relief sought and procedural context.

Considering a writ matter

The nature of the decision or action, the parties involved, the available record and other legal remedies may all be relevant. The appropriate forum, remedy and next steps require assessment of the particular facts and applicable law.

Legal assistance

Assistance is considered in context and is limited to the matter accepted on instruction. This overview does not determine whether a constitutional remedy is available in any individual case.
