Property Matters

Legal assistance with property disputes and transactions, including title verification, sale and purchase matters, lease issues and related litigation.

Overview

Property matters can involve questions about ownership records, transaction documents, possession or obligations under a lease. The practice includes property disputes, title verification and sale or purchase-related matters.

Matters and legal assistance

  • Review of title-related records and property documents.
  • Advice on sale, purchase and conveyancing documents.
  • Lease-related issues and disputes about contractual obligations.
  • Property litigation, including injunction-related matters where appropriate.

Document review and due diligence

A review is specific to the records supplied, the transaction or dispute, and the questions to be addressed. It does not amount to a guarantee of title or an assurance about facts that cannot be established from the available material.

Legal assistance

Advice may include identifying documents and issues for further review, explaining available legal options and, where instructed and appropriate, assisting with related proceedings. Property rights and remedies depend on the facts and applicable law.
